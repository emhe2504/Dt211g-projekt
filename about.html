About us

Hi there!

We, the team behind EuropaWiki, created this page so that you can easily find information about your travel destination in Europe. Maybe you just want to know what currency to exchange to or if there is a time difference at your destination. Don't worry, we'll help you.



The setup is simple. Search for a country in Europe and then you have the information.

Information you can find:

  • Capitals
  • Languages
  • Currencies
  • Population
  • Date, time and timezone
  • Weather

We are happy to have you here! Don't hesitate to contact us if you are missing any information on our site. AND! Don't forget to follow us on Instagram (Europa_Wiki).
